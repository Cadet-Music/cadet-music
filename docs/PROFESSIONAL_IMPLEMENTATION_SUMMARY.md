## Level Standards Framework

### BASIC Level - Foundation
**Focus**: Building fundamental skills and establishing proper technique

**Key Standards**:
- Scales: Bb Major, F Major at quarter = 60, 1 octave
- Tone: Clear, centered tone with basic control
- Rhythm: Quarter notes, 4/4 and 3/4 time, quarter = 60-100
- Intonation: Recognizes sharp/flat when told (±25 cents)
- Articulation: Basic tonguing, legato, staccato

**Critical Skills**:
- Proper embouchure formation
- Diaphragmatic breathing
- Correct posture and hand position
- Basic rhythm and timing

### LEVEL ONE - Developing Musician
**Focus**: Expanding technical ability and musical understanding

**Key Standards**:
- Scales: 5 scales at quarter = 66, both tongued and slurred
- Tone: Consistent, focused tone with improved control
- Rhythm: All standard note values including sixteenths, compound time
- Intonation: Adjusts when instructed (±20 cents)
- Articulation: Staccato, legato, accent, tenuto

**Critical Skills**:
- Expanded scale vocabulary
- Better tone control
- Understanding of musical structure
- Beginning sight reading

### LEVEL TWO - Competent Performer ⚠️ CRITICAL
**Focus**: Developing technical proficiency and musical maturity

**Key Standards**:
- Scales: 8 scales at quarter = 72, mixed articulations
- Tone: Rich, projecting tone with excellent control
- Rhythm: Complex rhythms, triplets, all time signatures
- Intonation: **CRITICAL** - Identifies and adjusts independently (±15 cents)
- Articulation: All standard articulations at fast tempos

**Critical Skills**:
- **CRITICAL intonation independence** - Must learn to hear and adjust pitch
- Technical proficiency
- Musical maturity
- Ensemble skills

### LEVEL THREE - Advanced Musician
**Focus**: Achieving technical mastery and musical sophistication

**Key Standards**:
- Scales: All scales (2 octaves) from memory at eighth = 60
- Tone: Beautiful, consistent tone with excellent control
- Rhythm: Complex syncopation, irregular meters
- Intonation: Excellent awareness, quick adjustments (±10 cents)
- Articulation: All articulations, complex combinations

**Critical Skills**:
- Technical mastery
- Musical sophistication
- Personal interpretation
- Two-octave scales from memory

### LEVEL FOUR - Proficient Performer ⚠️ CRITICAL
**Focus**: Demonstrating advanced technical command and artistic maturity

**Key Standards**:
- Scales: All scales (2 octaves) from memory at eighth = 72
- Tone: Refined, beautiful tone with exceptional control
- Rhythm: Complex rhythms, polyrhythms, changing meters
- Intonation: **CRITICAL** - Perfect in ensemble context (±8 cents)
- Articulation: Perfect control at all tempos and dynamics

**Critical Skills**:
- **CRITICAL ensemble intonation leadership** - Must lead and adjust ensemble tuning
- Artistic maturity
- Sight reading without assistance
- Professional-level musicianship

### LEVEL FIVE - Master Musician ⚠️ CRITICAL
**Focus**: Achieving comprehensive mastery and artistic excellence

**Key Standards**:
- Scales: All scales (2 octaves) from memory at eighth = 80
- Tone: World-class tone with complete control
- Rhythm: Extremely complex rhythms, polyrhythms, metric modulation
- Intonation: **CRITICAL** - Perfect in all contexts (±5 cents)
- Articulation: Perfect control at all tempos, dynamics, and styles

**Critical Skills**:
- **CRITICAL perfect intonation** - Must be flawless in all contexts
- World-class tone quality
- Virtuosic technique
- Artistic excellence
