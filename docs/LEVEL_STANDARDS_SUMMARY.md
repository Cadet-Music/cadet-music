## 🎯 Key Standards by Level

### BASIC - Foundation Level
**What you're building:** Fundamental skills and proper technique

**Measurable Goals:**
- ✅ Play 2 scales (Bb Major, F Major) at ♩=60
- ✅ Clear, centered tone with basic control
- ✅ Steady rhythm in 4/4 and 3/4 time
- ✅ Recognize when pitch is sharp/flat (when told)
- ✅ Basic articulation (tonguing, legato, staccato)
- ✅ Perform 2 prepared pieces from memory

**What sets BASIC apart:**
- First steps in music
- Focus on proper fundamentals
- Building confidence
- Learning to read music

---

### ONE - Developing Musician
**What you're building:** Expanded technical ability and musical understanding

**Measurable Goals:**
- ✅ Play 5 scales at ♩=66 (both tongued and slurred)
- ✅ Consistent, focused tone with improved control
- ✅ Accurate rhythm with all note values including sixteenths
- ✅ Adjust pitch when instructed (±20 cents)
- ✅ Execute all articulations cleanly
- ✅ Sight read up to 16 bars with assistance

**What sets ONE apart:**
- Expanded technical vocabulary
- Better tone control
- Understanding of musical structure
- Beginning sight reading

---

### TWO - Competent Performer ⚠️ **CRITICAL LEVEL**
**What you're building:** Technical proficiency and musical maturity

**Measurable Goals:**
- ✅ Play 8 scales at ♩=72 with mixed articulations
- ✅ Rich, projecting tone with excellent control
- ✅ Precise rhythm with complex patterns and triplets
- ✅ **CRITICAL:** Identify and adjust intonation independently (±15 cents)
- ✅ Clean articulation at fast tempos
- ✅ Good sight reading with accuracy

**What sets TWO apart:**
- **CRITICAL intonation skills** - Must learn to hear and adjust pitch independently
- Technical proficiency
- Musical maturity
- Ensemble skills

**Why Level 2 is Critical:**
This is where students transition from "learning to play" to "playing musically." The intonation requirement is CRITICAL because:
- Students must develop their ear
- They must learn to adjust pitch independently
- This skill is essential for ensemble playing
- Without this, higher levels are impossible

---

## 🎯 Critical Skills by Level

### Level 2 - CRITICAL: Intonation Independence
**What this means:**
- Can hear when you're out of tune
- Can adjust pitch quickly and accurately
- Can do this independently, not just when told

**Why it's critical:**
- Foundation for all higher levels
- Essential for ensemble playing
- Without this skill, you can't progress

**How to develop it:**
- Practice with tuner daily
- Play with ensemble and listen carefully
- Learn to hear beats (wavering sound when out of tune)
- Adjust pitch until beats disappear

---

### Level 4 - CRITICAL: Ensemble Intonation Leadership
**What this means:**
- Can lead ensemble tuning
- Can adjust to different tuning systems
- Can understand harmonic intonation
- Can maintain perfect intonation in context

**Why it's critical:**
- Professional-level skill
- Essential for leading sections
- Required for advanced ensemble playing

**How to develop it:**
- Play in ensemble regularly
- Listen to how your part fits harmonically
- Practice adjusting to different tuning systems
- Learn to lead section tuning

---

### Level 5 - CRITICAL: Perfect Intonation in All Contexts
**What this means:**
- Flawless intonation regardless of context
- Can adjust to any situation
- World-class musicianship
- Mastery level achievement

**Why it's critical:**
- Defines master-level playing
- Required for professional performance
- Demonstrates complete musical command

**How to develop it:**
- Practice in all contexts (solo, ensemble, different keys)
- Develop perfect ear for intonation
- Learn to adjust instantly
- Study with master teachers

## 🎯 Student Progression Example

### Moving from Level 1 to Level 2

**Current Skills (Level 1):**
- 5 scales at ♩=66
- Consistent tone
- Basic intonation (adjusts when told)
- Sight reading with assistance

**Required for Level 2:**
- 8 scales at ♩=72
- Rich, projecting tone
- **CRITICAL:** Independent intonation adjustment
- Good sight reading accuracy

**Focus Areas:**
1. Learn 3 new scales (Ab, Db Major + F# minor)
2. Increase scale tempo from ♩=66 to ♩=72
3. Develop richer tone quality
4. **CRITICAL:** Develop independent intonation skills
5. Improve sight reading accuracy

**Practice Recommendations:**
- Practice new scales daily with metronome
- Long tone exercises for tone development
- **CRITICAL:** Practice with tuner, learn to hear and adjust pitch independently
- Sight read one new piece daily
- Play with ensemble to develop intonation skills

**Timeline:**
- Months 1-2: Learn new scales, increase tempo
- Months 3-4: Focus on tone development and **CRITICAL intonation skills**
- Months 5-6: Refine sight reading, prepare for assessment

## 🔧 Implementation Details

### Helper Functions

**`getLevelStandard(level: CadetLevel): LevelStandard`**
- Returns complete standards for a specific level
- Includes all skill areas and measurable goals

**`checkLevelRequirements(level, studentSkills): AssessmentResult`**
- Checks if student meets level requirements
- Returns strengths, areas for improvement, and recommendations
- Identifies CRITICAL skills that must be met

**`getProgressionPath(fromLevel, toLevel): ProgressionPath`**
- Shows what skills need to be developed
- Provides focus areas and practice recommendations
- Helps students understand the journey ahead

### Integration Points

The level standards system can be integrated into:
- **Student assessment** - Evaluate readiness for next level
- **Practice recommendations** - Guide focused practice
- **Progress tracking** - Monitor skill development
- **Instructor guidance** - Identify areas for improvement
- **Goal setting** - Set clear, measurable goals
