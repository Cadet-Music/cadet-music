# Pre-Course and Articulation Implementation - Complete Summary

## Overview

Successfully implemented a comprehensive Pre-Basic course for absolute beginners and added essential articulation and phrasing lessons to the BASIC level. Also corrected drill terminology to match official standards.

## Key Changes

### 1. Pre-Basic Course (NEW)

Created a complete Pre-Basic level for students who have never played an instrument before.

**Pre-Basic Lessons (8 lessons):**

1. **Welcome to Music** (`pre-intro`)
   - What is music?
   - Why learn an instrument?
   - What to expect from your musical journey
   - Skills: Music appreciation, understanding purpose, motivation

2. **Instrument Care Basics** (`pre-instrument-care`)
   - Proper instrument handling
   - Basic cleaning procedures
   - When to seek professional help
   - Skills: Instrument handling, basic maintenance, respect for equipment

3. **Basic Posture** (`pre-posture`)
   - Standing position
   - Holding your instrument
   - Common posture problems
   - Skills: Standing posture, instrument holding, body alignment

4. **Breathing Basics** (`pre-breathing`)
   - Diaphragmatic breathing
   - Breath control
   - Breathing exercises
   - Skills: Diaphragmatic breathing, breath control, air support

5. **Embouchure Introduction** (`pre-embouchure`)
   - What is embouchure?
   - Basic embouchure formation
   - Common embouchure problems
   - Skills: Mouth position, lip control, facial muscles

6. **Making Your First Sound** (`pre-first-sound`)
   - Preparing to play
   - Making sound
   - Troubleshooting
   - Skills: Sound production, embouchure application, air stream control

7. **Assembly and Disassembly** (`pre-assembly`)
   - Assembly steps
   - Disassembly steps
   - Practice routine
   - Skills: Instrument assembly, proper handling, care routine

8. **Pre-Basic Assessment** (`pre-exam`)
   - What you've learned
   - Assessment checklist
   - Moving forward
   - Skills: Knowledge review, skill assessment, progress evaluation

**Pre-Basic Level Goal:** Introduction - Building foundational knowledge and basic skills for absolute beginners

**Next Level Prep:** Prepares students for BASIC level by establishing proper fundamentals

### 2. Articulation and Phrasing Lessons (NEW)

Added two essential lessons to BASIC level that were missing.

**Basic Articulation Types** (`basic-articulation`)
- Staccato: Short and detached
- Legato: Smooth and connected
- Accent: Emphasized and strong
- Combining articulations
- Skills: Staccato technique, legato playing, accent execution, articulation control
- Level Goal: BASIC Level Goal: Foundation - Developing articulation control
- Next Level Prep: Articulation skills are essential for musical expression and advanced techniques

**Musical Phrasing** (`basic-phrasing`)
- What is a phrase?
- Shaping a phrase
- Breathing and phrasing
- Practicing phrasing
- Skills: Phrase recognition, musical shaping, breath control, expression
- Level Goal: BASIC Level Goal: Foundation - Developing musical expression
- Next Level Prep: Phrasing skills are essential for musical interpretation and performance

---

### 3. Drill Terminology Correction (FIXED)

Corrected drill terminology to match official Canadian Cadet Organization standards.

**Changes Made:**
- ❌ "Parade rest" → ✅ "Stand at ease"
- Updated all references in lesson content
- Updated skills developed list
- Updated key points
- Updated practice instructions

**Correct Drill Positions:**
1. **Attention** - Formal position, heels together, stand tall
2. **Stand at ease** - Relaxed but formal position, left foot out
3. **Marking time** - Marching in place, knees to 6 inches
4. **Forward march** - Basic forward movement, 30-inch step

- Content: What is embouchure, formation, problems
- Interactive: Yes (mirror practice)

- Duration: ~25 minutes
- Content: Preparing, making sound, troubleshooting

- Content: Assembly steps, disassembly, practice
