- Many lessons had no detailed content in lessonContent.ts
- Students couldn't actually learn from the lessons

### 2. **Comprehensive Tuning Coverage**

#### BASIC Level: "Tuning Basics" 🎯
**New lesson added to both saxophone and clarinet**

Content includes:
- What is tuning? (Concert Bb standard)
- How to adjust your instrument (push/pull mouthpiece or barrel)
- Using a tuner (green = in tune, red = sharp/flat)
- Tuning different notes (low, middle, high registers)
- Daily tuning routine (5 minutes before every practice)

Key Points:
- Tune to concert Bb (written G for alto, C for tenor/clarinet)
- Push mouthpiece/barrel in to raise pitch (if flat)
- Pull mouthpiece/barrel out to lower pitch (if sharp)
- Make tiny adjustments (1-2mm at a time)
- Check multiple notes, not just the tuning note
- Tune every day before practice

Practice Instructions:
- Daily tuning routine with tuner
- Check low, middle, and high notes
- Adjust embouchure for consistently sharp/flat notes
- 5 minutes before every practice session
