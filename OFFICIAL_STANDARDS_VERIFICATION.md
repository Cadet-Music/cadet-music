### 2. ✅ Scale Requirements - VERIFIED

From Annex B (pages B1-2-1 to B1-2-3), the scale requirements for **Eb Instruments** (Alto/Baritone Saxophone):

#### Level BASIC (♩ = 60, Memorization NOT required, quarter notes)
- **Major:** G Major

#### Level ONE (♩ = 66, Memorization NOT required, quarter notes)
- **Major:** G, C, D
- **Chromatic:** G
- **Minor Harmonic:** E, A, B

#### Level TWO (♩ = 72, Memorization NOT required, quarter notes)
- **Major:** G, C, F, D, A
- **Chromatic:** G
- **Minor Harmonic:** E, A, D, B, F#

#### Level THREE (♪ = 60, Memorization REQUIRED, eighth notes)
- **Major:** G, C, F, Bb, D, A, E
- **Chromatic:** G
- **Minor Melodic:** E, A, D, B, F#, C#
- **Minor Harmonic:** E, A, D, B, F#, C#

#### Level FOUR (♪ = 72, Memorization REQUIRED, eighth notes)
- **Major:** G, C, F, Bb, Eb, D, A, E, B, Ab
- **Chromatic:** G
- **Minor Melodic:** E, A, D, B, F#, C#, G#, F
- **Minor Harmonic:** E, A, D, B, F#, C#, G#, F

#### Level FIVE (♪ = 80, Memorization REQUIRED, eighth notes)
- **Major:** G, C, F, Bb, Eb, Ab, Db, Gb, Cb, D, A, E, B, F#, C#
- **Chromatic:** G
- **Minor Melodic:** E, A, D, B, F#, C#, G#, D#, A#, D, A, F#, C#, A#
- **Minor Harmonic:** E, A, D, B, F#, C#, G#, D#, A#, D, A, F#, C#, A#

**For Bb Instruments (Tenor/Soprano Saxophone, Clarinet, Trumpet):**
- Similar structure but different starting keys
- Level BASIC: C Major (instead of G)
- All other levels follow same pattern but transposed

**Previous Issues:**
- Lessons only had questions, no teaching content
- No examples or practical guidance
- No practice instructions

**Enhancements Applied:**
- Created comprehensive lesson content for 16 lessons
- Each lesson includes:
  - Introduction explaining what will be learned
  - Multiple teaching sections with detailed explanations
  - Real-world examples
  - Practical tips
  - Key points summary
